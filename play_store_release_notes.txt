v1.0.9 Release Notes:

✨ What's New & Improved ✨
• Beautiful New Waveform: Upgraded the recording visualization to smooth, overlapping Siri-style waves.
• Perfected Voice Segmentation: Re-tuned speech-to-text timeouts so your sentences don't get cut off when you take a breath.
• Smart Spacing: Text is now injected seamlessly with automatic space formatting—no more mashed words!
• Live Text Preview: Dictating outside a text field? A sleek new scrollable bubble now shows your words in real-time as you speak.
• Clipboard Perfection: Copied text now safely accumulates into a single complete block when you finish recording, rather than overwriting itself.
• Setup UI Polish: Improved the Settings and Home Screen to clearly show Microphone permissions.
