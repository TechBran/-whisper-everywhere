## 8. Shipped as a real census family (same day)

The spike's branch was thrown away and the family built properly on
`feat/npu-8gen2-family`, on top of the 0.62.2 re-measurement:

- `qcs8550` is the **fifth census family** — packGroup `soc_qcs8550`, HTP v73, socModels
  `{SM8550, SM8550-AC}`, sharing 7 Gen 4's `libQnnHtpV73Skel.so` byte for byte.
- **Both tiers**, not turbo alone. The owner's ruling was that other models "stay hidden, just
  like we do on the CPU tier" — and on the CPU tier hidden rungs stay catalogued.
  `WhisperCatalog.ONE_TIER_ID` already narrows a capable device's chooser to `npu-turbo` alone,
  so the family inherits turbo-only *presentation* by existing. An earlier cut deleted the small
  artifact instead; `WhisperCatalogHelpersTest` refused it by name.
- Both packs **measured by the instrument**, reproducing the spike's hand-hashed digests with the
  metadata and graph-IO gates passing: `io-census EQUAL to the spec row` for both tiers.
- `SM8550` / `SM8550-AC` left `CPU_BY_CENSUS` with a dated note — the only exit that ledger
  allows, and this one device-executed rather than HEAD-verified alone.
- Device group added; the XML is generated from the census and pinned byte-for-byte.

### Two assertions that were true only by coincidence

Four families had four distinct HTP versions, so "one skel per family" and "one skel per
architecture" were the same sentence. The 8 Gen 2 is v73 exactly as the 7 Gen 4 is. Both counts
broke, and neither was relaxed:

- the distinctness test now asserts the **bijection** — families sharing a skel must share an
  architecture, and no skel may appear under two architectures, which is the actual hazard
  (a v73 blob staged for a v75 device);
- the skel-extraction table asserts one row per **architecture**, while the per-family loop still
  proves every family's skel is present.

`NpuGateTest` also used `SM8550` as its canonical "most likely false allow — everything about it
looks right". That part now correctly allows, so the role passed to the 8+ Gen 1 (`SM8475`), with
the 8 Gen 2 asserted as a TRUE allow beside it.

### The one thing NOT verified

**The AAB is now 6.63 GB.** 105 uploaded to production at 5.48 GB, so that size is known-good;
6.63 GB is not. Play caps an individual asset pack and also limits an app's totals, and whether
this crosses either is unconfirmed — the session's web-search budget was spent, so it was not
checked rather than checked and cleared. **Confirm before spending an upload on it.** If it does
cross, the lever is delivery scope rather than the census: no device needs more than one variant
of each pack, and 5.43 GB of the bundle is `npu_turbo`'s six variants.
