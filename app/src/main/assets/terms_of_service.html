2. Description of Service

Whisper Everywhere is a voice-to-text application that:

  • Provides a floating overlay interface for voice recording
  • Transcribes audio to text entirely on your device using the open-source whisper.cpp engine
  • Injects transcribed text into text fields or copies it to your clipboard
  • Detects media playback for optional transcription purposes

3. On-Device Processing and Model Downloads

The App performs all speech recognition locally on your device:

  • Your audio never leaves your device. Transcription is performed on your phone; no audio or transcribed text is transmitted to us or any third party.
  • You download a speech model once. The first time you choose a model, a model file is downloaded over HTTPS from the public Hugging Face repository so the App can work offline afterward.
  • No account or subscription is required and there are no per-minute usage fees for transcription.
  • Model files require device storage. Larger, higher-accuracy models require more storage and more device memory (RAM) to run well; you are responsible for choosing a model appropriate for your device.
