Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

Summary: Whisper Everywhere transcribes your speech 100% on your device by default. Your audio is processed locally by an on-device speech-recognition model and never leaves your phone unless you both add your own cloud provider key and select that provider as your transcription engine (see Section 6 — off by default, and entirely optional). There are no accounts, no servers we control, and no analytics or tracking. Other than an optional cloud provider you configure yourself, the only time the app uses the network is to download a speech model file once, so it can work fully offline afterward.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Whisper Everywhere ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application ("App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices. The App performs all speech-to-text transcription directly on your device using the open-source whisper.cpp engine.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Whisper Everywhere is built to keep everything on your device:

  • We do not collect your audio recordings. Your voice is captured and transcribed entirely on your device by default; audio is never uploaded anywhere unless you both add your own cloud provider key and select that provider as your transcription engine (see Section 6), and even then it goes only to that provider, never to us.
  • We do not collect your transcriptions. Transcribed text is produced on your device and stays on your device (or, if you use an optional cloud provider, is produced by that provider and returned to your device — see Section 6).
  • We do not collect personal information. There is no account creation, email address, or any personal identifier required to use the App.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs in the App.
  • We do not operate any server that receives your data. We have no backend that your audio or text passes through.

3. On-Device Processing

All speech recognition happens locally:

  • Audio from your microphone is converted to text by an on-device whisper.cpp model running on your phone's CPU.
  • Audio is held only transiently in memory during transcription and is not written to permanent storage or transmitted off the device.
  • Because processing is local, the App works fully offline once a model has been downloaded.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Speech Model Files: The whisper model you download is stored in the App's private storage so transcription can run offline.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as haptic-feedback preference, selected model, and the floating-bubble position.
  • Usage Statistics: Basic counts (e.g., number of transcriptions) stored locally for your own reference only.
  • Long-Form Transcripts: When you record long sessions, the running transcript is written to a private file in the App's storage so very long recordings do not exhaust memory. These files stay on your device and can be cleared by you.

5. Network Use

The App's only use of the network is to download a speech model file:

  • Model files are downloaded over HTTPS from the public Hugging Face model repository (huggingface.co) the first time you choose a model.
  • These are ordinary file downloads. No audio, transcription, or personal data is sent as part of the request.
  • After a model is downloaded, transcription works entirely offline and the App does not need a network connection to function.

By default, at no point is your audio or transcription data transmitted off your device. The only exception is the optional cloud providers feature described next, which is off until you deliberately turn it on.

6. Cloud Providers (Optional)

Off by default. Cloud transcription requires two separate, deliberate steps: adding your own API key for a provider, AND selecting that provider as your transcription engine in the App's Cloud Providers settings. On-device transcription remains fully functional — and the default — until you do both.

Adding a provider key (for OpenAI, Google Gemini, or ElevenLabs) sends only that key to the provider's own servers, once, to verify it works with your account — nothing else is sent at that point.

When you then select a provider as your transcription engine, the audio you dictate is sent to that provider's own servers, using your own account with them, so it can be transcribed. This happens only for the provider you have selected, and only while a key is saved for it. Nothing is sent to any provider until both steps are done, and switching back to on-device or removing the key stops all transmission to that provider immediately.

If the selected provider fails — a network error, an outage, a rejected request — the on-device model transcribes that audio instead, automatically, so your dictation still completes without you doing anything.

Cloud read-aloud is off by default. Read-aloud is produced entirely on-device unless you choose a cloud voice for a provider you have configured. When you select a cloud voice, the text you choose to have read aloud is sent to that provider's own servers, using your own account with them, to be spoken — only for the provider whose voice you selected, only while its key is saved, and only after you accept the updated disclosure. The on-device voice remains the default and takes over automatically if the cloud voice fails mid-read, so reading always completes.

Device audio captured for media transcription is never sent to a cloud provider, regardless of your settings. When you transcribe audio playing from another app (podcasts, videos, and similar, captured via Android's screen-capture/MediaProjection permission), that audio is always processed on-device — a cloud provider you have selected only ever receives audio you dictate yourself through the microphone, or an audio file you explicitly choose to transcribe with that provider.

You are using your own account. That provider bills you directly for usage, and your data is handled under that provider's own privacy policy and terms of service, not ours. We recommend reviewing each provider's policy before adding a key.

What each provider does with data you send them differs, and matters for this decision:

  • OpenAI: Does not train its models on data sent through the API.
  • Google Gemini: On the free tier, Google uses what you send to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Paid tiers do not.
  • ElevenLabs: Trains on API data by default; you can opt out in your ElevenLabs account settings.

Your provider API key is stored only on your device, encrypted using the Android Keystore. We never see it, log it, or transmit it anywhere except: once, to the provider you added it for, to verify it works, and thereafter — only while that provider is selected as your transcription engine — in the authenticated transcription request to that same provider.

7. Permissions We Request

The App requests the following permissions, used only for the stated purposes:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): To capture your voice for transcription. By default this is processed entirely on-device and never stored permanently or sent anywhere; it is sent to a cloud provider only if you have both added your own key for one and selected it as your transcription engine (see Section 6).
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): To show the floating bubble overlay that lets you dictate from any app.
  • Accessibility Service: To detect the focused text field and insert your transcribed text into it. The service only responds to text-field focus and performs text insertion when you dictate; it does not read, collect, or transmit the content of your screen.
  • Notification Access (Optional): To detect media playback for the optional media-transcription feature. This permission is optional and used only to detect when audio/video is playing; audio captured this way is always transcribed on-device and is never sent to a cloud provider (see Section 6).
  • Internet: Used to download the speech model file from Hugging Face, and — only if you have added your own key for a cloud provider and selected it as your transcription engine (see Section 6) — to send that key to the provider once to verify it, and to send your dictated audio to that provider for transcription. It is never used to send your data to us.
  • Vibration: To provide optional haptic feedback during recording (can be disabled).

8. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • By default, transcription is on-device, so your audio and text never traverse the internet and are not exposed to interception in transit.
  • If you add your own cloud provider key, requests to that provider use HTTPS, and your key is encrypted on-device using the Android Keystore (see Section 6).
  • Model downloads use HTTPS encryption.
  • Model files, preferences, and transcripts are kept in the App's private storage.
  • No data is stored on external servers or cloud services controlled by us — cloud providers you configure yourself are controlled by you, under your own account with them.

9. Children's Privacy

Whisper Everywhere is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we do not collect personal information from any users, this naturally extends to children as well.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

11. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Because we do not collect or store your data on any server, all of your data lives on your device and is fully under your control:

  • You can delete downloaded speech models from within the App's Settings.
  • You can clear the App's data from Android Settings to remove all locally stored preferences, models, and transcripts.
  • You can uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data.
